# The Group

Lasuarindo Group is an Indonesian business and investment group. The website introduces the group, the companies it works with and the district where many of them operate.

## What the website tells you about the group

- Its vision and mission, and the values it says it follows.
- A short statement about its strengths.
- Messages from its founder and commissioner.
- A timeline of four milestones. The details are still marked draft, so they should not be read as final history.
- The wider network of organisations the group works with.

## What the site covers

The About Us page is the place to read who the group is and how it sees its work. The Businesses page then shows what the group actually does.
