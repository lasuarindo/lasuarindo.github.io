# Working With Us

The website offers three ways to start a conversation with the group.

## The three options

- **Partnership** for organisations that want to build something together.
- **Investor relations** for people interested in investing in the group's businesses.
- **Collaboration** for institutions and communities that want to run joint activities.

## What each page tells you

Each page explains why the topic matters, who it suits, how a first conversation usually starts, and what kind of cooperation might follow.

Programme details are marked draft on each page. When you follow the contact link, the contact form opens with the subject already filled in. See the [draft label definition](/docs/guide-contributing#draft-labels).
