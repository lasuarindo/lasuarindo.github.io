# Legal and Privacy

This part of the website keeps the legal information together.

## Licensing

The licensing page shares the basic licence information the group is able to publish. It lists the Business Identification Number. Licence numbers, issuers and years are not shown because they have not been confirmed.

## Policies and questions

- The **legal index** collects the site's documents.
- The **privacy policy** explains what personal information is collected, how it is used, how it is protected, cookies and your rights.
- The **terms and conditions** set out general rules for using the site. This wording is general and needs legal review before it is treated as a binding agreement.
- The **FAQ** gives short answers to common questions and points to the contact page when more help is needed.

Some information is marked draft. See the [draft label definition](/guide/reading-a-page#draft-labels).
