# Businesses and T46

The group works in six areas of business and brings many of its companies together in one district called T46, in Bandung.

## The six areas

- Investment and ownership.
- Property and district management.
- Technology, including product-protection work.
- Media and creative work.
- Food, drink and consumer goods.
- Lifestyle, including a padel business.

## The district and its units

T46 is home to twelve business units. They are grouped into three categories: businesses managed directly, partnerships, and other businesses in the district ecosystem.

The website lets you browse the district, filter the directory and open a page for each unit. A future ecosystem is also listed, but it is clearly marked as a plan rather than a confirmed district.
