# Reading a Page

Different sections write pages in different ways. Knowing the shape helps you find what you need quickly.

## In the For Everyone section

These pages use everyday language. They describe what a visitor can see, what it is for, and where it links. They do not mention code, files or settings.

## In the Pages section

A page has two parts:

1. **The plain part** explains what the page does for a visitor.
2. **"For maintainers"** gives the technical details: the route, the copy bundle, the data it reads and any special behaviour.

This is why the Pages section is a combination of both styles. You can read only the first part if you want the simple explanation.

## In the Docs section

These pages assume you are working on the project. They use technical terms and point to files and configuration.

## Draft labels

When something is marked draft, it is still being prepared. Treat it as an illustration, not as a confirmed fact. Draft labels are intentional and should only be removed after the information is confirmed.
